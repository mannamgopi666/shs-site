04 — Document intelligence

M2D

Paper clutter in, clean financial records out.

Stack

  • Flutter
  • PHP
  • MySQL

The problem

Businesses and individuals drown in daily paper bills and printed receipts. Organising, filing and totalling expenses by hand is slow, error-prone, and makes tracking financial health close to impossible.

What we built

A cross-platform scanning and expense management system that reads both printed and handwritten receipts, extracts the critical fields into a secure central database, automates monthly expenditure totals and keeps a searchable history of spending.
